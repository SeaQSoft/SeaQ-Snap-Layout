How it works
Grab a window by its title bar and drag it up. The snap bar slides down; drop the window onto a zone and it fills exactly that area. Drag it away again and it gets its old size back.
Opening the settings editor
System Settings → Window Management → KWin Scripts → gear icon next to “SeaQ Snap Layout” → “Open Snap Layout settings editor” → OK.
