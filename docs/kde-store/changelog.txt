KDE Snap Layout 1.6.5 – First release on the KDE Store

- Snap bar at the top edge with six layouts, landing preview and size restore
- Settings editor: General, Appearance, Behavior & Animation, Layouts, About & Help
- Layout designer with up to 16 zones, eleven templates, divider snapping
- Colors: system / light / dark, system accent, swatches or color wheel
- Six motion curves, separate slide-in and slide-out durations
- Editor follows the system colors, window decoration and language (62 languages)
